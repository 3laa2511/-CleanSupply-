# CleanSupply — Reuse vs. Rebuild Matrix (Phase Zero Audit)

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Classification Key:**
- **KEEP:** Working, production-aligned, and directly reusable as-is or with trivial config adjustments.
- **MODIFY:** Sound foundation/UI/contracts that should be preserved and adapted to consume the new backend/auth flows.
- **REBUILD:** Architecturally unsafe, non-scalable, or fundamentally tied to client-direct Firestore authority; must be replaced.
- **REMOVE:** Unsafe or redundant client-side privileged logic / hardcoded demo scaffolding to be retired once replaced.
- **UNKNOWN:** Requires project-owner decision or third-party vendor selection before final engineering disposition.

## 2. Preservation Commitment

Roughly **65–70% of the existing Android Compose UI layer** (`AuthScreen`, `HomeScreen`, `CatalogScreen`, `CartCheckoutScreen`, customer tabs of `OrdersAccountScreen`, `ProductCard`, `ProductDetailSheet`, `CleanSupplyTheme`, `AppStrings`, and visual assets) is high quality and directly reusable without rewriting screens or navigation structure.
